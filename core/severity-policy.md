# Severity Policy

- **P0 Critical** — Immediate compromise, irreversible corruption, systemic outage, or protected baseline violation.
- **P1 High** — Release-blocking correctness, authorization, data integrity, migration, or repeatability defect.
- **P2 Medium** — Material degradation or maintainability risk with a bounded workaround.
- **P3 Low** — Minor defect, localized maintainability issue, or non-blocking improvement.
- **INFO** — Observation with no required remediation.

Severity measures impact and urgency. Confidence measures proof strength. They must never be collapsed into one field.
